# Lumidari

Website and digital pamphlet for Lumidari, an AI agency for local businesses in Visakhapatnam.

**Live page (GitHub Pages, until the .in domain is set up):** https://krishnasai-pentakota.github.io/Lumidari_AI/

To view locally, open `index.html` in a browser. Everything it needs is in this folder: the page, and the screenshots, recordings and videos in `media/`.

**To share as one file:** send `Lumidari-AI-agency.html` (7.4 MB) on WhatsApp or email. It has every picture, video and font built in and opens offline on any phone or computer.

## What's on the page

- Problems we solve: six common pain points, each flipping to what we build
- Case study: Sri Ratna's Homoeo Clinic (website, online booking, doctor's dashboard, waiting-room TV, print banner) with a 55-second build video
- Try it: the clinic's WhatsApp booking assistant, with its real replies and a sample patient
- TimberTrack for Aditya Traders: lorries, CFT and party ledger, with a 44-second "what it solved" video
- Concept: eye-test results to WhatsApp for optical shops
- Studio work, services, how we work, contact

All screens are recorded with sample patients, buyers and figures. No real customer data is in this repository.
