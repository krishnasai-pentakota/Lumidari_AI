TimberTrack for Aditya Traders

A timber trader in Andhra Pradesh sends mango and teak logs on lorries to buyers more than 1,500 km away in Rajasthan. Every lorry used to live on paper. We built one app for the yard staff, the accountant and the owner.

Designed after two months working in the timber yard alongside the team, so it follows the yard's own steps and words.

Yard, Andhra PradeshOn the wayBuyer, Rajasthan

Loading: CFT, weight, freight and invoice recorded before the lorry leaves

1,500+ kmper load, yard to buyer
3 rolesowner, accountant, 10 yard staff
1 recordper lorry, loading to payment
0 priceson staff phones

What it solved

  1. Lorry details spread over tally books and phone callsEvery lorry in one list: order taken, on the way, completed
  2. Freight, costs and dues added up by handCFT, weight, freight, costs and amount due on one screen
  3. “Where is the lorry now?” calls all dayOne tap asks the driver for his location on WhatsApp
  4. Nobody could say quickly who still owed moneyEach buyer's balance, worked out from sales and payments
  5. Prices visible to everyone at the yardStaff see trips and costs; prices never reach their phones
